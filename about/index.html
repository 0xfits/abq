About

A quantitative market-making firm

Abbey Quant provides continuous liquidity in digital asset markets, working systematically and within limits set in advance.

What we do

Abbey Quant is a quantitative market-making firm that provides continuous liquidity in digital asset markets. We quote prices systematically and absorb the flow that comes to them, so that counterparties can trade with less friction. The work is measured over long stretches of time rather than in single trades, and we size our activity to the risk we are prepared to carry. We do not promise outcomes, and we describe our business here only in general terms.

Principles

  • Risk-first

    Every decision begins with what can go wrong. Exposure is defined and bounded before opportunity is pursued.

  • Systematic

    Written rules and models, applied consistently, take the place of discretion at the point of execution.

  • Automation

    Repetitive work is automated so that human attention is reserved for oversight, exceptions and judgement.

  • Disciplined execution

    The process is followed through changing conditions; we do not chase a result by stepping outside it.
